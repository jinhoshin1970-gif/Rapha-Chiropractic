About Dr. Lee

A chiropractor who treats every patient with care and integrity

Meet Dr. Sangsoo Lee, DC

Dr. Sangsoo Lee is dedicated to providing high-quality chiropractic care to individuals and families throughout the Duluth community.

Rather than simply masking symptoms, he looks for the root cause of your pain and builds a personalized plan that supports long-term health. Patients appreciate his thorough evaluations, compassionate care, and commitment to lasting results.

As a Christian healthcare professional, Dr. Lee believes every patient should be treated with dignity, respect, honesty, and genuine compassion, whether you are managing chronic pain, recovering from an injury, or seeking preventive care.
